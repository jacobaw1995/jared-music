Bio Copy

Approved bios for event listings, press features, and venue marketing. Click to copy.

Jared Walker is a traditional country artist rooted in small-town life — born in California, raised on the open fields of Ohio. With a voice built for wide-open spaces and a natural stage presence, he performs country music the way it was meant to be made: honest, lived-in, and no shortcuts taken.

Influenced by Elvis Presley, Dwight Yoakam, George Strait, and Willie Nelson, Walker started performing at 17 alongside his father in The Dirty River Band. His music carries that roots DNA forward — high production quality married to real emotion. His debut single "Down On The River" and follow-up "Runnin'" are available on all major platforms.

Born in California, raised on the open fields of Ohio — Jared Walker carries both in his music. With a natural presence that commands a room and a voice built for wide-open spaces, he's the kind of performer who walks in looking like he belongs: worn denim, a flannel rolled to the elbows, boots that have seen some miles.

His father played in The Dirty River Band — blues, rock, country, all of it. At 17, Jared joined him on stage, picked up acoustic guitar, and found a voice that was unmistakably his own. He gravitated toward the artists who never faked it — Elvis Presley, Dwight Yoakam, George Strait, Willie Nelson. Songwriters who told the truth.

"Songwriting is a hundred percent therapy. When you let those emotions flow, that's when the real songs happen."

— Jared Walker

Rooted in small-town life — the kind of place where the work is hard, the people are real, and the music means something — Walker makes country without trends or shortcuts. His debut single "Down On The River" is an upbeat, 90s-flavored anthem born from summers spent fishing and boating. His follow-up single "Runnin'" builds on that sound, available now on all major platforms.

Logo Usage Rules

Keep the brand consistent — these apply to all assets.

✓ Do
  • Use the approved logo files as provided
  • Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the cap height
  • Use black on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds
  • Scale proportionally — never stretch or squish
  • Use the monogram when space is limited
✕ Don't
  • Recolor or apply effects to any logo
  • Place black logo on dark backgrounds (use white version)
  • Outline, shadow, or add gradients to the logo
  • Rearrange or combine logo elements
  • Use low-resolution screenshots in place of provided files
